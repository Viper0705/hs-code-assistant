Privacy policy

This notice describes the current HS Code Assistant setup and its project website. The publisher is Marco Passarini, reachable at marco@viper07.it.

Using the assistant

Product descriptions, uploaded documents and conversations used for classification are processed within the OpenAI services where you use the assistant. The publisher does not operate a separate backend or archive that receives or stores these classification inputs.

OpenAI’s processing, retention and available data controls depend on the service, account and settings you use. Consult OpenAI’s privacy policy and your account or organisation settings. This notice does not replace those policies or promise a particular OpenAI retention period.

Visiting this website

This is a static information website hosted on GitHub Pages. The website code does not add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, user accounts, contact forms or document uploads. GitHub may process technical request information to provide its hosting service under the GitHub privacy statement.

Contacting support

If you contact the publisher, the information you choose to send is used to handle your enquiry. This is separate from your assistant conversations. GitHub Issues are public and may show your username and the content you post. Email messages are processed through the email services involved.

Please send only information necessary for the enquiry. Do not submit confidential documents or personal data about other people.

Privacy questions and requests

Contact the publisher by email regarding information you have sent directly for support. Requests concerning OpenAI conversations should be addressed through OpenAI’s privacy channels; requests concerning GitHub-hosted account or issue data should also follow GitHub’s privacy procedures. Applicable privacy rights remain unaffected.

Changes

This notice will need to be reviewed if the assistant’s integrations or the website’s data practices change.

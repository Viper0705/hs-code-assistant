Examples of questions to ask

These are example prompts, not tested classification results.

  • Help me assess the customs classification of a wooden pallet. What product details do you need?
  • What information is needed to distinguish possible HS headings for a USB charger?
  • Help me review the classification of a polyester fabric using the specification I provide.
Support for your assessment.

Suggestions are indicative. They are not a binding customs decision or a substitute for professional review. National tariff subdivisions and the relevant date must be checked before using a code in a declaration.

Purpose

The assistant provides informational support for exploring possible customs classifications based on product descriptions and documents supplied by the user. Suggestions may be incomplete or incorrect.

Verification before operational use

Do not rely on an output alone when preparing a customs declaration or making a compliance decision. Verify the product facts, relevant jurisdiction, tariff version, applicable provisions and current official sources. Seek qualified advice or a binding decision from the competent authority where appropriate.

Provide accurate information and use only content you are authorised to share. Avoid unnecessary personal data and confidential material. Do not use the assistant to support false declarations or other unlawful activity.
